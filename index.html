SELF-SERVICE ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete Your BazaarLab Account & Data

App Name: BazaarLab (com.bazaarlab)
Developer: NPL Studios

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your BazaarLab account and all associated account and local data immediately from inside the app:

  1. Open the BazaarLab Android app and sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Funds tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the Legal, Privacy & Disclosure section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete Forever. (If your login session is older than a few minutes, the app will prompt you to Sign Out and sign back in first for security.)

Option 2: Delete On-Device Paper Trading Data Only

If you want to keep your account login but erase local holdings, positions, orders, watchlists, trade history, price alerts, and preferences on your device, open Funds → Legal, Privacy & Disclosure → Erase All On-Device App Data.

Option 3: Request Deletion Without the App

If you have already uninstalled BazaarLab and want us to delete your account and associated records, send an email from the address registered with your BazaarLab account to:

Email: info.nplstudios@gmail.com
Subject: BazaarLab Account Deletion Request

Verified email deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Your sign-in profile, account identifier, email address, display name, virtual balance preferences, and notification token.
  • Inbox Notices & Support Tickets: All personal inbox messages and all customer support tickets and chat messages linked to your account.
  • On-Device Records (In-App Flow): All locally stored orders, positions, holdings, custom watchlists, trade history, price alerts, and app preferences.

Data Retention

Account deletion is permanent and immediate when performed in-app. No personal profile, support history, or virtual trading data is retained after deletion is completed.
